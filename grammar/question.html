? 疑问句

口语 Part 1 考官问、Part 2 你来问：疑问句语序错一次，流利度分就下去了。

01 一般疑问句与简略回答A1 基础 把 be 动词、助动词（do/does/did/have）或情态动词提到主语前。简略回答用同一个助动词：Yes, I do. / N… 2 条避坑 · 2 题

把 be 动词、助动词（do/does/did/have）或情态动词提到主语前。简略回答用同一个助动词：Yes, I do. / No, she isn't.

  • Are you from Shanghai? — Yes, I am.你是上海人吗？——是的。
  • Did they win the match? — No, they didn't.他们赢了比赛吗？——没有。
  • Can you come tomorrow? — Yes, I can.你明天能来吗？——能。
实义动词直接提前
Like you pizza? Do you like pizza?
实义动词的疑问句要借助助动词 do/does/did。
简略回答用错助动词
Does he play golf? — Yes, he plays. Does he play golf? — Yes, he does.
简略回答要用与问句相同的助动词。

02 特殊疑问词A2 核心 疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序。How 组合很多：How old / How much / How many / How long / … 2 条避坑 · 8 题

疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序。How 组合很多：How old / How much / How many / How long / How often / How far。

疑问词问什么例
What time时刻What time does the film start?
Where地点Where do you live?
When时间When is your birthday?
Who人Who is your best friend?
Whose谁的Whose phone is this?
Which哪一个（有范围）Which bus goes to the airport?
Why原因Why are you late?
How long多长时间 / 多长How long does the journey take?
How often频率How often do you play tennis?
How far距离How far is it to the beach?
  • How much does this T-shirt cost?这件 T 恤多少钱？
  • What did you do last weekend?你上周末做了什么？
疑问词后用陈述语序
Where you went yesterday? Where did you go yesterday?
特殊疑问句 = 疑问词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形。
How long / How often 混用
How long do you go to the gym? — Three times a week. How often do you go to the gym? — Three times a week.
问次数/频率用 How often；问时长用 How long。

03 对主语提问（Who / What 作主语）A2+ 衔接 PET 当 who / what 本身就是主语时，不用助动词，直接接动词（注意三单/过去式）。 2 条避坑 · 3 题

当 who / what 本身就是主语时，不用助动词，直接接动词（注意三单/过去式）。

  • Who lives in that house?谁住在那栋房子里？
  • What happened at school today?今天学校发生了什么？
  • Who did you see? ← I saw Tom.（对宾语提问，要用 did）你看见了谁？
主语疑问句加 did
Who did break the window? Who broke the window?
who 是主语，直接接过去式 broke，不加 did。
What happened 写成 What did happen
What did happen to your bike? What happened to your bike?
what 作主语，直接用 happened。
