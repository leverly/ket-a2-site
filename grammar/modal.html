◈ 情态动词

口语 Part 2 与写作邮件里提建议、请求、邀请都离不开 can / should / would like。

去练语法真题 →
01 can / could：能力、请求、许可A1 基础 can 表示现在的能力、请求或许可；could 表示过去的能力，或更礼貌的请求。后接动词原形，没有三单变化。 2 条避坑 · 5 题

can 表示现在的能力、请求或许可；could 表示过去的能力，或更礼貌的请求。后接动词原形，没有三单变化。

  • I can swim, but I can't dive.我会游泳，但不会潜水。
  • When I was five, I could ride a bike.我五岁时就会骑自行车了。
  • Could you tell me the way to the station, please?请问您能告诉我去车站怎么走吗？
can 后加 to
She can to speak French. She can speak French.
情态动词后接动词原形，不加 to。
can 加三单 -s
He cans play the piano. He can play the piano.
情态动词没有人称变化。

02 must / have to / mustn't / don't have toA2 核心 must 和 have to 都表示「必须」；mustn't 表示「禁止」；don't have to 表示「不必」。过去的「必须」… 3 条避坑 · 6 题

must 和 have to 都表示「必须」；mustn't 表示「禁止」；don't have to 表示「不必」。过去的「必须」用 had to。

形式含义例
must / have to必须You must wear a helmet. / I have to get up early.
mustn't禁止，不准You mustn't run in the corridor.
don't have to不必，没必要You don't have to pay. It's free.
had to过去必须We had to wait for an hour.
  • Students must bring a pencil to the exam.考生必须带铅笔参加考试。
  • It's Saturday, so I don't have to go to school.今天周六，所以我不用上学。
mustn't 与 don't have to 混淆
You mustn't come if you are busy.（想说"不必来"） You don't have to come if you are busy.
mustn't = 禁止；don't have to = 不必。意思完全不同。
have to 的三单
He have to finish his homework. He has to finish his homework.
have to 有人称变化，三单用 has to。
must 的过去式
Yesterday I must stay at home. Yesterday I had to stay at home.
must 没有过去式，过去的"必须"用 had to。

03 should：建议A2 核心 should / shouldn't 表示建议「应该 / 不应该」。询问建议：What should I do? 2 条避坑 · 3 题

should / shouldn't 表示建议「应该 / 不应该」。询问建议：What should I do?

  • You should bring a warm jacket. It's cold in the mountains.你应该带件暖和的外套，山里很冷。
  • You shouldn't eat so many sweets.你不应该吃那么多糖。
should 后用 to
You should to see a doctor. You should see a doctor.
should 后接动词原形。
should 后用 -ing
We should going now. We should go now.
情态动词后不能接 -ing。

04 may / might：可能性A2 核心 may 和 might 表示「也许，可能」（不确定）。may 还可以用于礼貌请求许可：May I…? 2 条避坑 · 2 题

may 和 might 表示「也许，可能」（不确定）。may 还可以用于礼貌请求许可：May I…?

  • Take an umbrella. It might rain later.带把伞吧，晚点可能会下雨。
  • I may go to the cinema tonight, but I'm not sure.我今晚可能去看电影，但还不确定。
  • May I use your phone?我可以用一下你的手机吗？
may be 与 maybe 混用
Maybe it will rain. → It maybe rain. It may rain. / Maybe it will rain.
maybe 是副词（也许），放句首；may 是情态动词，后接原形。
might 后加 to
She might to come. She might come.
情态动词后接动词原形。

05 邀请与提议：would like / Shall I…? / Would you like…?A2 核心 would like = 想要（比 want 礼貌）；Would you like (to)…? 用于邀请；Shall I/we…?… 2 条避坑 · 5 题

would like = 想要（比 want 礼貌）；Would you like (to)…? 用于邀请；Shall I/we…? 用于主动提出帮忙或提议。写作 Part 6 邮件高频！

功能句型例
礼貌地想要I'd like + 名词 / to doI'd like a glass of water.
邀请Would you like to + V?Would you like to come to my party?
提出帮忙Shall I + V?Shall I carry your bag?
一起做的提议Shall we + V? / Let's + V / Why don't we + V?Shall we meet at 3?
  • Would you like to go to the beach with us on Sunday?周日想和我们一起去海边吗？
  • Shall I open the window?要我把窗户打开吗？
Would you like 后接原形
Would you like come with me? Would you like to come with me?
would like 后接 to do。
回答 Would you like…? 用 Yes, I would like.
— Would you like some tea? — Yes, I would like. — Would you like some tea? — Yes, please. / Yes, I'd love some.
接受邀请或物品用 Yes, please / I'd love to；would like 后要有宾语。
