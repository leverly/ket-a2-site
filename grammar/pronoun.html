01 人称代词与物主代词A1 基础 主格作主语，宾格放动词或介词后；形容词性物主代词后面跟名词，名词性物主代词单独使用。 3 条避坑 · 7 题

主格作主语，宾格放动词或介词后；形容词性物主代词后面跟名词，名词性物主代词单独使用。

主格宾格形容词性物主名词性物主反身
Imemyminemyself
youyouyouryoursyourself
hehimhishishimself
sheherherhersherself
ititits—itself
weusouroursourselves
theythemtheirtheirsthemselves
  • Can you help me with my homework?你能帮我做作业吗？
  • Is this pen yours or mine?这支笔是你的还是我的？
  • Be careful! Don't hurt yourself.小心！别伤到自己。
介词后用主格
This present is for she. This present is for her.
介词 for 后用宾格 her。
their / there / they're 混淆
The students forgot there books. The students forgot their books.
their = 他们的；there = 那里；they're = they are。
名词性物主代词后加名词
That is mine bag. That is my bag. / That bag is mine.
mine 不能修饰名词；名词前用 my。

02 指示代词 this / that / these / thoseA1 基础 this/these 指近处，that/those 指远处；this/that 接单数，these/those 接复数。打电话时用 … 2 条避坑 · 3 题

this/these 指近处，that/those 指远处；this/that 接单数，these/those 接复数。打电话时用 This is… 介绍自己。

  • These shoes are too small. Can I try those ones?这双鞋太小了，我能试试那双吗？
  • Hello, this is Anna. Can I speak to Ben?你好，我是安娜。能请本接电话吗？
单复数不一致
This apples are delicious. These apples are delicious.
apples 是复数，用 these。
电话里用 I am
Hello, I am Tom speaking. Hello, this is Tom (speaking).
英语电话自我介绍习惯用 This is…。

03 不定代词 some- / any- / every- / no-A2 核心 something / somebody / somewhere 多用于肯定句和提供、请求；anything / anybody /… 3 条避坑 · 6 题

something / somebody / somewhere 多用于肯定句和提供、请求；anything / anybody / anywhere 用于否定和疑问；everything 全部；nothing / nobody 本身含否定。它们作主语时谓语用单数；形容词放在后面（something nice）。

物人地点
some-somethingsomebody / someonesomewhere
any-anythinganybody / anyoneanywhere
every-everythingeverybody / everyoneeverywhere
no-nothingnobody / no onenowhere
  • I'm hungry. I want something to eat.我饿了，想吃点东西。
  • Is there anything interesting on TV tonight?今晚电视上有什么好看的吗？
  • Everyone was happy at the party.聚会上每个人都很开心。
形容词放前面
I want to buy interesting something. I want to buy something interesting.
修饰不定代词的形容词要后置。
nothing / nobody 再加否定
I didn't see nobody. I didn't see anybody. / I saw nobody.
英语不用双重否定。
everyone 后用复数
Everyone are here. Everyone is here.
everyone / everybody 作主语谓语用单数。
