✎ 动词形式与基本句型

写作 Part 6 邮件最常用的句型：There is…、I'd like to…、I enjoy -ing…。

去练语法真题 →
01 There is / There areA1 基础 表示「某处有某物」。be 动词的单复数由后面紧跟的名词决定；过去时用 there was / there were。 3 条避坑 · 6 题

表示「某处有某物」。be 动词的单复数由后面紧跟的名词决定；过去时用 there was / there were。

There is + 单数/不可数 ｜ There are + 复数 ｜ Is there…? / Are there…?
  • There is a big park near my house.我家附近有个大公园。
  • There are three bedrooms in our flat.我们的公寓有三间卧室。
  • There was a lot of snow last winter.去年冬天下了很多雪。
用 have 表示"某处有"
In my town has a big cinema. There is a big cinema in my town.
"某地有某物"用 there be；have 表示"某人拥有"。
复数名词用 is
There is two cafés in the street. There are two cafés in the street.
后面是复数名词 cafés，用 are。
不可数名词用 are
There are some milk in the fridge. There is some milk in the fridge.
milk 不可数，按单数处理。

02 祈使句与 Let'sA1 基础 用动词原形开头表示命令、建议、指路；否定用 Don't + 原形；提议一起做用 Let's + 原形。 2 条避坑 · 6 题

用动词原形开头表示命令、建议、指路；否定用 Don't + 原形；提议一起做用 Let's + 原形。

  • Turn left at the bank and go straight on.在银行左转然后直走。
  • Don't forget your umbrella!别忘了带伞！
  • Let's meet outside the cinema at seven.我们七点在电影院外面见吧。
Let's 后加 to
Let's to go swimming. Let's go swimming.
let 后跟不带 to 的动词原形。
否定祈使用 not 开头
Not touch the paintings. Don't touch the paintings.
否定祈使句用 Don't + 动词原形。

03 have got / haveA1 基础 have got 和 have 都表示「拥有」，英式口语常用 have got。注意它们的否定和疑问形式不同。 2 条避坑 · 6 题

have got 和 have 都表示「拥有」，英式口语常用 have got。注意它们的否定和疑问形式不同。

have gothave
肯定She has got a dog.She has a dog.
否定She hasn't got a dog.She doesn't have a dog.
疑问Has she got a dog?Does she have a dog?
  • I've got two brothers and a sister.我有两个哥哥和一个姐姐。
  • Have you got any pets?你养宠物吗？
两种结构混用
Do you have got a pen? Have you got a pen? / Do you have a pen?
have got 的疑问把 have 提前；have 的疑问借助 do。不能混合。
三单用 have got
He have got blue eyes. He has got blue eyes.
第三人称单数用 has got。

04 动词 + to do / -ingA2 核心 有的动词后面接 to do，有的接 -ing，必须成组记忆。介词后面一律接 -ing。 3 条避坑 · 10 题

有的动词后面接 to do，有的接 -ing，必须成组记忆。介词后面一律接 -ing。

接 to do接 -ing两者都可
want, would like, decide, hopeenjoy, finish, mindlike, love, hate
need, learn, plan, trygo + -ing（go swimming）start, begin
forget, agree, offer介词后：good at / interested in / thanks forprefer
  • I want to learn to play the guitar.我想学弹吉他。
  • My dad enjoys cooking at the weekend.我爸爸周末喜欢做饭。
  • Thanks for inviting me to your party!谢谢你邀请我参加派对！
enjoy 后接 to do
I enjoy to play tennis. I enjoy playing tennis.
enjoy 后面只能接 -ing。
want 后接 -ing
She wants going to the zoo. She wants to go to the zoo.
want 后接 to do。
would like 后接原形
I'd like go to the museum. I'd like to go to the museum.
would like 后接 to do。

05 被动语态（一般现在 / 一般过去）A2+ 衔接 PET 当动作的承受者是主语时用被动。KET 只考两种：am/is/are + 过去分词，was/were + 过去分词。动作执行者用 by… 2 条避坑 · 6 题

当动作的承受者是主语时用被动。KET 只考两种：am/is/are + 过去分词，was/were + 过去分词。动作执行者用 by 引出。

现在 am/is/are + done ｜ 过去 was/were + done ｜ (by + 执行者)
  • English is spoken in many countries.很多国家说英语。
  • This bridge was built in 1890.这座桥建于 1890 年。
  • The book was written by a young girl.这本书是一个年轻女孩写的。
漏掉 be
The house built last year. The house was built last year.
被动语态 = be + 过去分词，be 不能省。
用过去式代替过去分词
The window was broke. The window was broken.
break - broke - broken，被动要用过去分词 broken。
