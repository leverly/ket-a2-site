⇄ 介词

in / on / at 是 KET 每套必考的"送分题"，也是中国考生最容易随手写错的地方。

01 时间介词 in / on / atA1 基础 at + 钟点/具体时刻；on + 星期/日期/具体某天；in + 月份/年份/季节/上午下午晚上。 3 条避坑 · 9 题

at + 钟点/具体时刻；on + 星期/日期/具体某天；in + 月份/年份/季节/上午下午晚上。

介词用于例
at钟点、noon/night、the weekend（英）、Christmasat 7 o'clock, at night, at the weekend
on星期、日期、某天的上下午、生日on Monday, on 5 May, on Friday evening, on my birthday
in月份、年份、季节、the morning/afternoon/eveningin July, in 2024, in winter, in the morning
  • The concert starts at half past seven.音乐会七点半开始。
  • My birthday is on 3rd March.我的生日是 3 月 3 日。
  • We go skiing in winter.我们冬天去滑雪。
具体某天用 in
See you in Saturday. See you on Saturday.
星期几用 on。
night 用 in the
I can't sleep in the night. I can't sleep at night.
固定搭配 at night；in the morning/afternoon/evening。
next/last/this 前加介词
I'll see you on next Monday. I'll see you next Monday.
next、last、this、every 前不加 in/on/at。

02 地点介词 in / on / at / under / next to …A1 基础 in 在…里面（城市、国家、房间）；on 在…表面（墙上、桌上、楼层）；at 在某个点（车站、学校、家）。方位：under, beh… 3 条避坑 · 6 题

in 在…里面（城市、国家、房间）；on 在…表面（墙上、桌上、楼层）；at 在某个点（车站、学校、家）。方位：under, behind, in front of, next to, between…and…, opposite, near。

  • My flat is on the third floor.我家公寓在三楼。
  • The bank is opposite the post office.银行在邮局对面。
  • I'll wait for you at the bus stop.我在公交站等你。
楼层用 in
We live in the fifth floor. We live on the fifth floor.
楼层用 on。
arrive 后加 to
We arrived to London at noon. We arrived in London at noon.
arrive in + 城市/国家；arrive at + 车站、机场等小地点；不用 to。
go home 加 to
I went to home after school. I went home after school.
home 在这里是副词，前面不加 to。

03 方向介词与指路A2 核心 to（到）, into（进入）, out of（出来）, across（横穿）, along（沿着）, past（经过）, thro… 2 条避坑 · 6 题

to（到）, into（进入）, out of（出来）, across（横穿）, along（沿着）, past（经过）, through（穿过）, up/down（上下）, over（越过）。指路：go straight on, turn left/right, take the second turning。

  • Go along this street and turn right at the traffic lights.沿这条街走，到红绿灯右转。
  • Walk past the church and the museum is on your left.走过教堂，博物馆就在你左手边。
  • The cat jumped out of the box.猫从箱子里跳了出来。
across 与 through 混淆
We walked across the forest. We walked through the forest.
across 表示从表面一边到另一边（马路、河）；through 表示从内部穿过（森林、隧道）。
get on/in 混用
Get in the bus quickly! Get on the bus quickly!
公交、火车、飞机用 get on/off；小汽车、出租车用 get in/out of。

04 高频介词搭配A2 核心 形容词/动词 + 固定介词，必须整体记忆。介词后面跟名词、代词或 -ing。 3 条避坑 · 9 题

形容词/动词 + 固定介词，必须整体记忆。介词后面跟名词、代词或 -ing。

搭配含义
be good at擅长
be interested in对…感兴趣
be afraid of害怕
be famous for以…闻名
be different from与…不同
be late for…迟到
look at / look for / look after看 / 寻找 / 照顾
listen to听
wait for等待
talk to / about和…谈 / 谈论
think about / of考虑；想起
pay for为…付钱
thanks for感谢…
on foot / by car / by train步行 / 开车 / 坐火车
on holiday在度假
at home / at school / at work在家 / 在校 / 在上班
  • I'm not very good at maths.我数学不太好。
  • Can you look after my cat this weekend?这周末你能帮我照顾猫吗？
listen 漏 to
I like listening music. I like listening to music.
listen 是不及物动词，接宾语要加 to。
wait 漏 for
Please wait me here. Please wait for me here.
wait for sb 是固定搭配。
介词后用原形
She is good at draw. She is good at drawing.
介词 at 后接 -ing。
