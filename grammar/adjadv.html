01 比较级与最高级A2 核心 两者比较用「比较级 + than」；三者及以上用「the + 最高级 + in/of」。单音节加 -er/-est，多音节用 mor… 4 条避坑 · 11 题

两者比较用「比较级 + than」；三者及以上用「the + 最高级 + in/of」。单音节加 -er/-est，多音节用 more/most。

类型原级比较级最高级
单音节talltallerthe tallest
以 e 结尾nicenicerthe nicest
辅元辅big / hotbigger / hotterthe biggest / hottest
辅音 + yhappy / easyhappier / easierthe happiest / easiest
多音节expensivemore expensivethe most expensive
不规则good / wellbetterthe best
不规则badworsethe worst
不规则farfurther / fartherthe furthest
  • My brother is taller than me.我哥哥比我高。
  • This is the most beautiful beach in the country.这是全国最美的海滩。
  • The film was better than the book.电影比书好看。
双重比较
This bag is more cheaper. This bag is cheaper.
已经加 -er 就不能再加 more。
good 的比较级
Her English is gooder than mine. Her English is better than mine.
good 的比较级是不规则的 better。
最高级漏 the
He is tallest boy in our class. He is the tallest boy in our class.
形容词最高级前要加 the。
than 写成 then
Cats are smaller then dogs. Cats are smaller than dogs.
than = 比；then = 然后。

02 as … as / not as … asA2 核心 as + 原级 + as 表示「和…一样」；not as + 原级 + as 表示「不如…」。中间用原级，不用比较级。 2 条避坑 · 3 题

as + 原级 + as 表示「和…一样」；not as + 原级 + as 表示「不如…」。中间用原级，不用比较级。

  • My bike is as fast as yours.我的自行车和你的一样快。
  • The second film wasn't as good as the first one.第二部电影不如第一部好看。
as…as 中间用比较级
She is as taller as her mum. She is as tall as her mum.
as…as 中间用形容词原级。
as…than 混用
It's not as cold than yesterday. It's not as cold as yesterday.
as…as 是固定结构，不能换成 than。

03 频率副词及其位置A1 基础 always (100%) > usually > often > sometimes > hardly ever > never … 2 条避坑 · 3 题

always (100%) > usually > often > sometimes > hardly ever > never (0%)。位置：放在实义动词前，be 动词/助动词后。问频率用 How often…?

  • I usually have cereal for breakfast.我早餐通常吃麦片。
  • She is never late for school.她上学从不迟到。
  • How often do you go swimming? — Twice a week.你多久游一次泳？——一周两次。
频率副词放 be 动词前
He always is happy. He is always happy.
频率副词放在 be 动词之后。
放实义动词后
I play often tennis. I often play tennis.
频率副词放在实义动词之前。

04 形容词 vs 副词（quick / quickly）A2 核心 形容词修饰名词或放在 be/look/feel/sound 等系动词后；副词修饰动词，说明动作怎样做。大多副词 = 形容词 + -l… 3 条避坑 · 7 题

形容词修饰名词或放在 be/look/feel/sound 等系动词后；副词修饰动词，说明动作怎样做。大多副词 = 形容词 + -ly；不规则：good → well，fast → fast，hard → hard，early → early。

  • She is a careful driver. She drives carefully.她是个谨慎的司机，她开车很小心。
  • You speak English very well!你英语说得真好！
  • The soup smells delicious.这汤闻起来很香。（系动词后用形容词）
动词后用 good
He plays the piano very good. He plays the piano very well.
修饰动词 play 要用副词 well。
系动词后用副词
You look happily today. You look happy today.
look（看起来）是系动词，后接形容词。
hard 写成 hardly
She works hardly.（想说"努力"） She works hard.
hard 本身是副词"努力地"；hardly = 几乎不。

05 -ed / -ing 形容词A2 核心 -ed 形容词描述人的感受（bored, excited, interested, tired, surprised）；-ing 形… 2 条避坑 · 6 题

-ed 形容词描述人的感受（bored, excited, interested, tired, surprised）；-ing 形容词描述事物的特点（boring, exciting, interesting, tiring, surprising）。

  • The film was boring, so I was bored.电影很无聊，所以我觉得很没劲。
  • I'm really excited about the school trip.我特别期待这次学校旅行。
描述人的感受用 -ing
I am very interesting in science. I am very interested in science.
"我感兴趣"是人的感受，用 interested。
描述事物用 -ed
The match was very excited. The match was very exciting.
比赛本身令人激动，用 -ing。

06 too / enough / veryA2+ 衔接 PET too + 形容词（太…，带负面结果）；形容词 + enough（足够…）；enough + 名词（足够的…）。very 只表示程度… 2 条避坑 · 3 题

too + 形容词（太…，带负面结果）；形容词 + enough（足够…）；enough + 名词（足够的…）。very 只表示程度「很」。

  • This coat is too big for me.这件外套对我来说太大了。
  • He isn't old enough to drive.他年龄不够，不能开车。
  • Have we got enough chairs for everyone?我们的椅子够每个人坐吗？
enough 放形容词前
The water isn't enough warm. The water isn't warm enough.
enough 修饰形容词时要放在形容词后。
too 与 very 混用
The weather is too nice today! The weather is very nice today!
too 含有"过度、不好"的意思；单纯夸赞用 very。
